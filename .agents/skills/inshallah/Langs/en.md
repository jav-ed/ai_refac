# inshallah in English

## Placement

inshallah slots between the auxiliary and the main verb.

- "I will inshallah answer you."
- "We can inshallah focus on that next."
- "You should inshallah see the difference."

For usage patterns with English examples, see the [main guide](../SKILL.md).

## Negation (Case 5)

English wraps negation into the contracted auxiliary (can't, won't, doesn't, isn't). inshallah goes before that contracted form.

- "Pricing inshallah can't change overnight."
- "The deployment inshallah won't break anything."
- "The site inshallah won't go down during the launch."

Wrong position inverts the meaning: "Pricing can't inshallah change" reads as "hopefully impossible to change," not "hopefully won't change."
