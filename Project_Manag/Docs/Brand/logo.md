# Logo

The mark is the moving tile: a solid eight-point star (the khatam, two squares of one size, one turned by 45 degrees) with one gold square lifted just above its top point. A piece moves and the whole stays valid, which is what `refac` does. The star is the Islamic geometric form; there is no calligraphy or figure in it on purpose, because a mark gets used on stickers, avatars, and screenshots.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `refac-mark.svg` | The logo. Transparent, works on light and dark backgrounds (no neutral color in it). README, docs home page. |
| `refac-favicon.svg` | Same idea with a larger tile and gap, tuned to stay readable at 16 px. Browser tab icon. |
| `refac-mark-512.png`, `refac-avatar-512.png` | Raster copies: transparent, and on a dark square for the GitHub avatar. |
| `refac-doorway.svg` | The illustration for large empty spaces (docs home page, social preview): a tile passes up through a pointed arch. Not a logo and not for small sizes. |

The logo is the only mark that goes where an icon is needed (favicon, avatar, header, README). The doorway shares its two ingredients, the teal shape and the gold tile, so the two read as one family.

Colors: teal `#1D9E75` (star), gold `#EF9F27` (tile). Keep the gap between tile and star; do not recolor the tile to match the star.

The SVGs are generated from exact geometry (star tips and inner corners on one circle pair), so edit the numbers and regenerate instead of nudging points by hand. The docs site copy of the favicon is `public/favicon.svg` in the docs repository.
